FAQ

        

          

            Which favicon sizes do I need?
            

              16, 32 and 48 for browsers, 180 for Apple touch icon, and 192/512 for Android and PWA
              manifests.
            

          

          

            Is my logo uploaded?
            

              No. Everything is drawn on a canvas in your browser.
            

          

          

            Where do I put the files?
            

              Unzip into your site root and paste the HTML snippet inside <head>.
            

          

          

            Do I need a .ico file?
            

              Modern browsers accept PNG favicons. Only legacy browsers need favicon.ico.
            

          

          

            Why use a rounded shape?
            

              It is purely style. Rounded and circle shapes look softer in browser tabs; apple-touch
              icons are masked by iOS anyway.
