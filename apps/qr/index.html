FAQ

How does a UPI QR code work?

It encodes a upi://pay link with your UPI ID, name, optional amount and note. Any UPI app (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM) can scan it.

Is it safe to enter my UPI ID?

Yes. The QR is generated entirely in your browser and nothing is uploaded. A UPI ID is meant to be shared to receive money.

Will the logo break scanning?

Keep the logo small; it covers a limited area and error correction recovers the rest. Always test-scan before printing.

How does the WiFi QR work?

Phones read the WIFI: format and offer to join the network automatically.

What barcode formats are supported?

Code128, which supports all ASCII characters. It needs internet once to load the barcode library.
